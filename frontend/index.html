U.S. medical bills are vague by design. Candorify surfaces potential discrepancies to review — duplicate charges, arithmetic slips, and odd quantities — and translates confusing billing codes into plain English.

Free to use — just create an account. No card required.

🔍 Check engine

Detects duplicate lines, arithmetic errors, unusual quantities, and total mismatches. Every finding is a possibility for you to confirm — never an automatic verdict.

🌐 Translation

Turns CPT/HCPCS codes into plain English, and into other languages. Powered by NLM Clinical Tables with an offline fallback.

✉️ Templates

Editable letters to request an itemized bill or to ask a provider to review specific charges.

How we differ from an auditor

Candorify covers what auditors can't profitably touch. If reviewing your bill helps you catch a $60 questionable charge, that's cheaper and faster than hiring a professional — while still leaving the final call to you, your provider, and your insurer.
